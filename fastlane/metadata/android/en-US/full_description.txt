HandWrite turns your own handwriting into a font, on the device, without the network.

* Take one to five photos of a page you wrote.
* The app finds the letters, learns their shapes and builds a handwriting atlas.
* Type any text (Russian or English) and the app lays it out the way you write: slanted, joined
  letters, pen pressure, slight wobble.
* Export A5 PDF and print, share or keep it.

Everything is computed on the phone with classic computer vision and a 94 KiB neural network.
The app has no INTERNET permission.

Features:
* no template sheets required - ordinary notes work;
* cursive joins follow the actual pen movement of your letters;
* several variants of every letter, alternated automatically;
* ligatures for pairs you wrote joined together;
* English words are learned even on a Russian page, and vice versa;
* PDF export is vector, so it prints cleanly at any size.

Open source, GPL-3.0, no trackers, no ads, no telemetry.
